## Why this question keeps coming up

Ask ten business owners in Kenya what eTIMS is for, and half of them will say the same thing: *"it's a way for KRA to spy on you."* That rumour has followed eTIMS from its earliest days — and in 2026 it has grown teeth, mixing with stories about nil returns, M-Pesa balances and bank accounts.

It is a real, common concern. Like most fears around tax, it contains a small piece of truth wrapped in a bigger misunderstanding. So let's state the truth plainly:

**KRA does not watch your phone, your computer or your WhatsApp. What KRA sees is what goes into KRA's own systems — your eTIMS invoices, your filings, and the third-party records that tax law already allows it to collect.**

This page goes line by line: what eTIMS transmits, what KRA can check, and what it genuinely cannot.

## What KRA actually receives when you issue an eTIMS invoice

When you issue an invoice through eTIMS — app, web portal, USSD or your POS system — KRA receives the **invoice itself**:

- Your business name and KRA PIN
- The invoice number, date and time of issue
- What you sold: description, quantity, unit price
- The tax charged (VAT or simplified invoice amounts)
- The buyer's KRA PIN — **for B2B sales only**. For B2C (individual) sales, no buyer PIN is required
- A validation code/QR link that ties the invoice to KRA's records

That is the entire list. There is no hidden channel taking anything else. eTIMS is an **invoicing system, not a monitoring program** — the invoice that goes out is the invoice KRA gets back.

## What KRA can honestly see from elsewhere

The "they know everything" energy has a real basis — but it's built from **declarations and statutory records**, not surveillance:

- **Your own filings**: everything you have ever put on iTax — returns, amendments, obligations
- **eTIMS data**: every invoice transmitted — yours and your suppliers'
- **Withholding tax certificates**: when clients withhold 5% / 10% on your invoices, KRA holds that certificate file
- **Customs and import records**
- **Employment records** (PAYE)

None of this is secret — it's the tax records Kenya always kept, now joined up. What changed in 2026 is that KRA actively **cross-references** these sets during filing: eTIMS invoices vs declared sales, WHT certificates vs declared income, nil returns vs eTIMS activity. That's the real muscle — and it only bites when there's a gap between what you declared and what the records show.

## Your bank account and M-Pesa: what KRA cannot get — and what it can

- Your private messages, apps or browsing history
- The contents of your phone — no eTIMS app reads other apps or files
- Your M-Pesa statements "live" — mobile-money records sit with the operator; KRA's Commissioner General has stated in plain terms: *"We have no direct access to M-Pesa wallets, unless it is on a case-by-case basis where there is fraud or investigations"* (September 2025). A finance bill proposal in 2025 that would have let KRA demand bank/M-Pesa records without a court order was rejected by Parliament — the statutory route remains: targeted, case-by-case, for investigations.
- The song on your playlist. (Because some of these rumours need a little comedy.)

The "spy on your computer" version is false. eTIMS transmissions are invoices only — get any skilled developer, auditor or tax advisor to trace exactly what KRA's e-invoicing protocol carries and they will come back with items on the first list and nothing else.

## M-Pesa: the rumour that hurts the most

The rumour that spread hardest in early 2026: *"KRA will tax the money in my M-Pesa / bank account."*

Let's be accurate:

| What people say | What's actually true |
| --- | --- |
| "KRA taxes everything in my M-Pesa" | **False.** Money moving through M-Pesa is not income. Balances are not taxable. |
| "Any cash I receive means I owe tax" | **False.** Tax applies to **income** — gifts, family transfers, savings, own-account movements are not income. |
| "KRA sees my M-Pesa in real time" | **False.** KRA validates against the records it lawfully collects — filings, eTIMS, WHT, customs. Mobile money sits with the operator; KRA can obtain records on a case-by-case statutory basis (fraud or investigation), which is not the same as a silent live tap on every wallet. |
| "The nil-return suspension means I'm about to be assessed on money I moved" | **Half true** — and it's the half that matters. The suspension was aimed at people **declaring no income while the records say otherwise**. If you issued eTIMS invoices, held WHT certificates, or have customs records, that's what they check. M-Pesa balances alone aren't the target — undisclosed income is. |

So the thing that protects a business here is one habit above all: **file what is true.** The risk is never "a big M-Pesa balance." The risk is a return that contradicts the records that already exist in KRA's systems.

### The nil-return moment that freaked Kenya — what changed in January 2026

In January 2026 KRA suspended nil-return filing while it reviewed records, then reopened it for the 2025 filings after March 31, 2026 with validation checks embedded. KRA's own tally: 392,162 taxpayers who had taxes withheld from them in 2025 still filed nil for 2024. That caused real anxiety — people with active businesses who never report. The correct reading: nil returns now have to actually be nil. If you traded or issued invoices in the year, nil won't work; if you genuinely had no income, nil is still your obligation and filing it right keeps you clean.

## The line that replaces the fear

*"If it is not in eTIMS, it did not happen for KRA's purposes."*

That sentence works both ways:

- An expense without an eTIMS receipt = not claimable (your deduction "didn't happen").
- A sale never invoiced = a gap between KRA's records and your return — the thing audits look for.

The system punishes **gaps**, not small businesses. The entrepreneur whose invoices, records and returns match — who asks for their PIN on receipts and declares what they earned — is exactly the taxpayer the system treats best. Fear that pushes people off the system only creates the gaps that the system catches.

## FAQ

**Q: Does eTIMS let KRA read my phone or computer?**
No. It collects the invoice data you enter — nothing else, no permissions to other apps, no keystroke capture, no screen reading. This rumour is as old as the system itself, and it's false every time.

**Q: Will KRA tax the money in my M-Pesa or bank account?**
No. Money in an account is not income. What's taxable is income earned while declaring nothing against records KRA holds. File truthfully and you're covered by the systems.

**Q: I issued eTIMS invoices last year — can I still file a nil return in 2026?**
Not without risking a mismatch. If you transacted, file the return accordingly. Below the thresholds, "filing" something honest still usually means little or no tax — the compliance that matters is filing the right thing.

**Q: How do I know what KRA holds on me before I file?**
Log in to iTax and check your PRE-FILED data — you may see what KRA already has (withheld amounts, imported data). That's exactly the tool the compliance push is built on: look, reconcile, file.
