## Why corporate clients insist on eTIMS invoices — and why they won't wait

Your client's accounts team isn't being difficult. Their own compliance depends on your invoice:

1. **Your cost is only deductible with an eTIMS invoice.** Your client deducts what they pay you from their profits. Under the rules as they stand, that deduction is only valid if the supporting invoice went through eTIMS. Without it, the expense is **rejected in their own return** — so they literally cannot afford a non-eTIMS invoice from you.
2. **Input VAT fails without it.** To claim input VAT on your supply, they must hold a valid eTIMS invoice. Wrong format, no claim — it's that direct.
3. **Their Tax Compliance Certificate depends on a clean chain.** TCCs now attach to compliance across the supply chain, and a supplier with no eTIMS is a red mark in any audit conversation.

That's why clients send near-identical notices to everyone they buy from: *"All invoices must be eTIMS-compliant."* And over time, it extends from corporate buyers to anyone who invoices a taxpayer who files.

## What happens when you don't have it — and what it costs

- **Rejection, re-invoice, delay.** Your invoice bounces, the money stalls, and the same service quietly switches to a supplier who can produce the invoice.
- **Quiet loss of repeat work.** Clients don't usually fire you — they just stop placing work. The invoice is the passcode to their systems, and there's nothing personal in it: a format requirement, applied equally.
- **The pattern is public.** In April 2024 a Nairobi car wash operator told the Daily Nation his corporate business had fallen from roughly 20 cars a day to a handful after clients began demanding compliant invoicing. The exact figures don't matter — the lesson does. Small businesses lose more from a few lost corporate accounts than from any penalty letter.

## How to fix it — the five-day path (start today)

You do not need an expensive system. Realistic steps:

1. **Day 1–2 — Know where you stand.** Log in to iTax. VAT-registered and not yet on eTIMS? Onboard now — eTIMS Lite (web/USSD) is free and handles a handful of invoices a month, which covers most service businesses.
2. **Day 2–3 — Issue one real invoice.** Generate an invoice for a genuine low value transaction; inspect it, and if you can, have an advisor review it once. Most owners overestimate how hard this is.
3. **Day 3–4 — Tell your clients.** Write to the corporate clients that matter: *"We've onboarded eTIMS — invoices from [date] carry eTIMS invoice numbers."* One honest line rebuilds months of trust.
4. **Day 5 — Update your documents.** Add the statement to quotes and invoice templates. From day six, it's habit.

And run the habit on the other side too: **when you buy for the business — fuel, stationery, internet, tools — ask for an eTIMS receipt with your PIN.** You can't claim your side cleanly while your suppliers' invoices stay paper.

## What not to do — the mistakes that cost suppliers the account

- **Don't wait for the second reminder.** The first reminder is usually days before the first rejection, and the fix takes days. Act on the first.
- **Don't submit paper or till invoices as replacements.** The mismatch between invoice types is exactly what the rejection cycle detects.
- **Don't assume "this is KRA again, it will pass."** It hasn't and it won't — every finance function in the country is now validating against the real system in real time. The cheapest strategy in Kenya today is to get ahead of the standard.

## FAQ — "I'm small, do I really need eTIMS for one client?"

**Q: I'm a small business — do I really need to issue eTIMS invoices to a big client?**
Yes — especially if your client claims expenses or input VAT on what you supply. Size doesn't exempt you from their accounting. eTIMS Lite is the right level for small suppliers.

**Q: My client wants eTIMS invoices but I haven't onboarded yet. Can I backdate?**
No — eTIMS invoices can't be backdated. The right move is onboard now (it is fast) and issue everything from today; a client prefers an honest "we're onboarding now" to re-issued paper.

**Q: I'm a sole proprietor / consultant. Is this mandatory for me?**
If you're in business, yes. Any client withholding tax or claiming an expense on your invoice needs that invoice to go through eTIMS. Onboarding is quick when your iTax details are in order.

**Q: Do I need special invoicing software?**
No — eTIMS Lite (free) or the app covers it. High-volume businesses graduate to POS/API integration later; it's a volume decision, not an entry requirement.

**Q: Do I put the client's KRA PIN on my invoice?**
Yes, for B2B: the client's PIN goes on the invoice so they can claim input VAT. For B2C, no buyer PIN is needed. Those two rules carry the whole system.

**Q: My client is VAT-registered but I'm not — which invoice type is correct?**
When the two parties' VAT statuses don't match, the invoice type to use usually follows the non-VAT side's rules — confirming the right combination takes 2 minutes with the KRA support or your advisor, and prevents a batch of rejected invoices.
