📚 KRA Legacy Ledger Reconciliation Guide

Update: When KRA migrated to iTax, many old balances were transferred incorrectly. Here's how to check and fix yours.

What Is the Legacy Ledger?

Before iTax, KRA used an older system (often called the "legacy system"). When iTax launched, many taxpayer balances were migrated over — but not always correctly.

Common issues include:

  • Balances that were already paid showing as outstanding
  • Credit balances from overpayment not carried forward
  • Penalties that should have been waived still showing
  • Double-counted payments from manual reconciliation errors

If your legacy balance is real and unpaid, the 2026 Tax Amnesty may waive penalties and interest — check eligibility.
