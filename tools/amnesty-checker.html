About the KRA Tax Amnesty 2026

The KRA tax amnesty (Section 37E, Tax Procedures Act) waives 100% of penalties and interest on tax debt accumulated up to 31 December 2025, provided the principal tax is fully paid by 31 December 2026.

KSh 140 billion has already been waived for over 3 million taxpayers since the programme began.

What's Covered

  • Value Added Tax (VAT)
  • Pay As You Earn (PAYE)
  • Corporation Tax

  • Turnover Tax (TOT)

Frequently Asked Questions

When is the deadline?
31 December 2026. Act by October 2026 to allow time for reconciliations and payment plans.
Do I need to apply for the waiver?
No separate application is needed. Once you pay the principal in full, KRA's system automatically writes off the associated interest and penalties.
Can I pay in instalments?
Yes. KRA accepts payment plans of up to 18 months for material debts. Apply in writing through iTax.
What if I only pay part of the principal?
Partial payment does not unlock a partial waiver. The principal must be paid in full for each tax period.
Will I get a Tax Compliance Certificate after?
Yes. Once the amnesty payment is processed, you can apply for a TCC immediately — usually issued within 24-48 hours.
