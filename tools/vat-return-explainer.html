📄 Pre-Filled VAT Return Explainer

Since January 2024, KRA auto-populates your VAT return with data from eTIMS and customs. Here's how to read it — and what to double-check.

Line-by-Line Breakdown

Output VAT (Sales) Comes from eTIMS invoices you issued this period. Verify: total sales match your records. If you issued credit notes, ensure they're reflected.
Input VAT (Purchases) Comes from supplier eTIMS invoices in your name. Verify: each invoice has a valid QR code and control unit number. If a supplier didn't use eTIMS, you cannot claim that input VAT.
Import VAT Comes from customs declarations. Verify: the entry number and amount match your customs documents. Delayed entries may appear in a later period.
VAT on Zero-Rated Supplies KRA may pre-fill this if your eTIMS invoices include zero-rated items. Verify: you have documentary proof (e.g., export documents) for each zero-rated sale.
VAT on Exempt Supplies Not taxable. Verify: your exempt supplies are correctly classified. Charging VAT on exempt supplies means you must remit it.

⚠️ Common Pitfalls

  • Missing input VAT — if your supplier's eTIMS invoice wasn't transmitted to KRA, it won't appear. You must follow up with the supplier.
  • Duplicated entries — KRA's system may pull the same invoice twice. Cross-check the control unit numbers.
  • Wrong period — invoices issued in a different month may appear in the wrong return period. Check the invoice dates.
